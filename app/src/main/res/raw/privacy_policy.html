The short version

Darts Coach hears your score, works out what to throw, and tells you. The speech recognition, the darts knowledge, and the coach's voice all run on your phone. Your microphone audio is never sent anywhere.

The app has one network connection: to Google Play, to take payment for the optional Pro upgrade. That connection carries no microphone audio and no information about how you play.

What the app accesses

When you use Darts Coach, the app uses the microphone so it can hear the score you say. It also plays the coach's suggestions through the phone's speaker or your headphones.

The app saves the following on the device:

  • your settings, such as the double you finish on and any hit rates you have set yourself;
  • your hit counts, which is what the coach uses to work out the routes that suit you;
  • your game history and the statistics worked out from it, such as your average turn and the share of legs you have won.

This information stays on the device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its app data in Android settings, deletes it.

How microphone audio is used

Audio captured from the microphone is processed in memory on the device by the speech recogniser bundled in the app. It is used only to turn your spoken score into text and to calculate the coach's reply.

Audio is not uploaded, transmitted, sold, shared, or used for advertising. It is not retained after recognition. No code in the app reads the microphone audio and sends it anywhere.

The Pro upgrade

Pro is a one-time purchase, handled by Google Play. The app uses the Google Play Billing Library, which connects to the Play Store on your device to take the payment and to check what your account has already bought.

The purchase is handled entirely between your device and Google Play. The app does not see or store your payment details, and there is no account of ours to sign in to. Google Play holds the purchase, so the app can restore it if you reinstall or move to another device by asking Play what your account owns.

A cached record of whether you have bought Pro is kept on your device so that the app still works when the Play Store cannot be reached.

What the app does not do

Darts Coach has:

  • no account or sign-in;
  • no ads;
  • no analytics or telemetry;
  • no crash reporting that leaves the device;
  • no advertising, analytics, or cloud-storage SDKs.

The app does declare the Android INTERNET permission, and it is used for one thing: talking to Google Play about the purchase. The coach itself needs no network at all and works with the phone in aeroplane mode.

Data sharing and third parties

Darts Coach does not share user data with third parties. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or cloud-storage SDKs. Its speech-recognition and speech-synthesis libraries run locally and do not transmit data.

The Google Play Billing Library talks to Google Play. That is the same connection the Play Store app itself uses.

Data retention and deletion

Microphone audio is held only long enough to recognise a spoken score and is not retained afterwards.

Your settings, hit counts and game history are stored on the device only. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its app data in Android settings, deletes them. The app has no server-side copy and no account to delete.

The Pro purchase itself is recorded by Google Play, not by this app. It is handled under Google's own privacy policy, and it can be refunded from the Play Console.

Security

Because no audio is transmitted and there is no server of ours, the app does not encrypt data in transit and stores no cloud data. The purchase is encrypted by Google Play. Android's own app sandbox and permission controls protect the microphone access and locally stored settings and statistics.

Children

Darts Coach is a general-audience training utility. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy
