Darts Coach Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Darts Coach is an independent Android application. It is developed by Darts Coach.

The short version

Darts Coach hears your score, works out what to throw, and tells you. The speech recognition, the darts knowledge, and the coach's voice all run on your phone. Your microphone audio is never sent to a server, because the app does not have permission to use the internet.

What the app accesses

When you use Darts Coach, the app uses the microphone so it can hear the score you say. It also plays the coach's suggestions through the phone's speaker or your headphones.

The app may save game state on the device, such as the remaining score and the number of turns, so that a leg can continue after the app is closed. This information stays on the device.

How microphone audio is used

Audio captured from the microphone is processed in memory on the device by the speech recogniser bundled in the app. It is used only to turn your spoken score into text and to calculate the coach's reply.

Audio is not uploaded, transmitted, sold, shared, or used for advertising. It is not retained after recognition.

What the app does not do

Darts Coach has:

  • no account or sign-in;
  • no ads;
  • no analytics or telemetry;
  • no crash reporting that leaves the device;
  • no internet permission in its Android manifest.

The absence of the Android INTERNET permission is a technical property of the app, not only a policy: the app has no networking capability.

Data sharing and third parties

Darts Coach does not share user data with third parties. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or cloud-storage SDKs. Its speech-recognition and speech-synthesis libraries run locally and do not transmit data.

Data retention and deletion

Microphone audio is held only long enough to recognise a spoken score and is not retained afterwards.

Game state is stored on the device only. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its app data in Android settings, deletes it. The app has no server-side copy and no account to delete.

Security

Because all processing happens on the device and no audio is transmitted, the app does not encrypt data in transit and stores no cloud data. Android's own app sandbox and permission controls protect the microphone access and locally stored game state.

Children

Darts Coach is a general-audience training utility. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form will be updated before the changed version is published.
